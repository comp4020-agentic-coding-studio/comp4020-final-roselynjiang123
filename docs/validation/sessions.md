## S0 — v1, before the redesign (informal)

- **When:** November 2026, before the redesign
- **Version:** v1, with placeholder medicines and five fields to compare
- **Who:** me and my housemate (P1), who has a non-nursing background
- **What happened:** She understood what the game was for and how to play it, but not what it would actually do for anyone, and she found it boring.
- **What it changed:** This, together with my own look at the app as a nurse, led to the v2 redesign (crit 10).

## S1 — v2, before the new stylesheet (informal, before `plan.md`)

- **When:** 12 November 2026
- **Version:** v2 after Round C, run locally, still using the v1 stylesheet
- **Who:** non-nursing student peers (number and pairing not recorded)
- **Duration and completion:** not recorded
- **Conversation before signing:** not recorded
- **Where they got stuck:**
  - Why two people check independently, and why it matters, wasn't clear to them.
  - They weren't sure the medical information had credible sources or was realistic.
  - Moving back and forth between the records and the answer controls made it easy to forget what they had just read.
- **What they said** (paraphrased from my notes, not quotes): explain why two checkers are needed; make the sources easier to check; less scrolling and better proportions; maybe a panel for questions about each case, or optional hints.
- **Not covered:** a nursing audience, clinical accuracy, and whether they checked independently.
