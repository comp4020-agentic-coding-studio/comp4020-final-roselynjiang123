Independent double check

Second Pair of Eyes

You and another learner are a checking pair for six fictional medication records. Each of you checks on your own; neither sees the other's answer until both have signed. Then you review together: did you agree, and were you right?

How to play

  1. Open this site in two Chrome profiles or two browsers. An incognito window works but forgets who you are when it closes.
  2. In the first, choose Create a table, then Copy invite link.
  3. Open the link in the second and choose Join. Check each case on your own; both answers appear once you have both signed.

Six short cases. No timer, no leaderboard.

What the records show

The prepared record has two discrepancies: the patient is SIM-073 instead of SIM-037, and the time is 20:00 instead of 08:00. Finding one discrepancy does not end the check: this record had two.

Either checker can move you both on.

Pair review sentences (brief §6, first that applies): "Both checks were complete and correct." / "Checker A's check was complete; Checker B's was not." / "You both missed the same discrepancy: Dose." / "Together you found every discrepancy, but each check on its own was incomplete." / "Neither check was complete."
