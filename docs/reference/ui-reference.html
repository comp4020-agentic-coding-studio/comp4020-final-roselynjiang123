State 1 · Landing ( / )

Second Pair of Eyes

Two people check the same medication record on their own. Neither sees the other's answer until both have submitted. Then you compare: did you agree, and were you right?

How to play

  1. Open this site in two Chrome profiles or two browsers. An incognito window works but forgets who you are when it closes.
  2. In the first, choose Create a table, then Copy invite link.
  3. Open the link in the second and choose Join. Check each case on your own; both answers appear once you have both submitted.

Six short cases. No timer, no leaderboard.
